Case: State of New Mexico ex rel. Raúl Torrez, Attorney General v. Darren K. Indyke, et al., No. 1:26-cv-03543 (D.N.M.), filed 21 October 2026.
Defendants: Darren K. Indyke (individual + capacity as Executor of the Estate of Jeffrey E. Epstein and Administrator of the 1953 Trust); Richard D. Kahn (same capacities); Estate of Jeffrey E. Epstein; THE 1953 TRUST.
Judge: United States Magistrate Judge Gregory J. Fouratt (referred); John F. Robbenhaar also noted.
Claims: Federal Trafficking Victims Protection Act (18 U.S.C. §§ 1591–1595) and New Mexico Racketeering Act. Parens patriae action seeking to prevent Indyke and Kahn from profiting from the alleged enterprise, including blocking receipt of the $50 million and $25 million bequests respectively, and recovery of damages for survivors. Indyke received more than $16 million (2011–2019) plus a $7 million unrepaid loan and other benefits; Kahn received more than $10 million in the same window plus payments to HBRK Associates. Both are described as current trustees/administrators of the 1953 Trust and co-executors. Zorro Ranch (Stanley, NM) is central to the jurisdictional and factual allegations. Recent docket activity limited to notices of association for outside counsel (Motley Rice LLC attorneys).
Sources: CourtListener RECAP PDF of ECF 1; PACER Monitor public docket; contemporaneous reporting by Reuters, Guardian, CBS, CNN, Forbes, New Mexico DOJ press release, Santa Fe New Mexican, and others. All allegations remain unadjudicated.
