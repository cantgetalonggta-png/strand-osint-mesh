## Executive Summary
On 21 October 2026, New Mexico Attorney General Raúl Torrez filed *State of New Mexico ex rel. Raúl Torrez v. Darren K. Indyke et al.*, No. 1:26-cv-03543 (D.N.M.). Defendants include Darren K. Indyke and Richard D. Kahn (individual + executor/administrator capacities), the Estate of Jeffrey E. Epstein, and **THE 1953 TRUST**.
